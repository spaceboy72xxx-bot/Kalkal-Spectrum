# ⚡ KALKAL VIDEO - Studio

> **An in-browser, high-performance Music Visualizer & Video Generator built with pure HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, and vanilla JavaScript.**

---

## 🌟 Overview

**KALKAL VIDEO Studio** allows creators, music producers, and DJ remixers to generate stunning music videos and visualizers directly inside the browser—no heavy video editing software required.

It features **multi-instrument adaptive audio detection**, allowing specific elements (drums, bass guitar, electric guitars, vocals, and cymbals) to dynamically drive canvas animations in real-time.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌋 1. Multi-Instrument Reactive Spectrum
- **Dark Obsidian Lava (Pulang Magma):** Dynamically reacts, expands, and forms molten cracks based on individual instrument loudness (Kick, Toms, Guitars, Snares, Cymbals).
- **Elemental Lava Themes:** Neon Style, Dark Magma, Ice Glacial, and Sand Dune.
- **Classic Styles:** NCS Spikes, Trap Nation Dual, Smooth Liquid Blob, Bottom Equalizer, Avee Dots, Hexagon, and Sunburst.

### 📻 2. 5 Retro & Vintage Spectrum Styles
- 📻 **Dual Analog VU Meters:** Realistic swinging needle ballistics with peak danger zones.
- 📟 **Hi-Fi Segmented LED Blocks:** 80s stereo equalizer with floating, gravity-drop peak dots.
- ⚡ **Winamp Neon Oscilloscope:** Authentic green phosphor cathode tube waveform with grid.
- 🌆 **80s Synthwave Horizon Grid:** 3D perspective wireframe horizon with a neon sunset.
- 💿 **70s Spinning Vinyl Grooves:** Rotating vinyl record with specular sheen and sound groove pulse.

### 📹 3. Dynamic Camera & Beat Effects
- 📼 **90s VHS Tape Glitch:** Authentic tracking lines and RGB chromatic aberration.
- 📺 **Retro CRT TV Pulse:** Scanline barrel distortion and phosphor squeeze.
- 📻 **Vintage Vinyl Flutter:** Micro-rotational pitch wobble and warmth breathing.
- 🪩 **80s Disco Ghost Echo:** High-energy neon afterimage strobe.
- 🕯️ **Tube Amp Pulse:** Smooth organic double-beat vacuum tube warmth.
- 🔥 **Action Beats:** Bass Punch, Earthquake Shake, Strobe Flash, and Wild Combos.

### 📝 4. Beat-Reactive Caption & Typography
- **Customizable Text:** Add song titles, remix labels, lyrics, or artist shoutouts.
- **7 Unique Text Designs:**
  - 💎 Neon Cyber Cyan
  - 🔥 Fiery Magma Lava
  - 👑 3D Metallic Gold
  - 🌆 80s Retro Synthwave
  - ⚡ RGB Hologram Glitch (Chromatic Split)
  - 🤍 Minimalist Glass Badge
  - 🖤 Streetwear Bold Stroke
- **Beat Reaction Modes:** Bass Punch & Pop, Glow Flash, Wobble Tilt, Glitch Jitter, or Static.
- **Flexible Controls:** Real-time font size and vertical Y-position sliders.

### ✨ 5. Particles & Pyro Engine
- **Motion Physics:** Neural Synapse (laser threads), Celestial Rings, Deep-Sea Bio, Radial Burst, 3D Warp, Rain, and Embers.
- **Particle Shapes:** Glowing Nodes, Shimmer Stars, Music Notes, and Geometric Diamonds.
- **Pyro & Fireworks:** Logo Fireball blasts, fountain fireworks rockets, and diagonal bursts.

### 🎬 6. Lag-Free Export & YouTube-Ready Video
- **Smooth 30 FPS / 2.5 Mbps Recording:** Optimized bitrate prevents mobile browser lag and frame drops.
- **YouTube 0:00 Duration Fix:** Integrated with `fix-webm-duration` to patch EBML metadata headers—ensuring YouTube displays the full, accurate video length with a functional seekbar.
- **Mobile Friendly:** One-click direct download and native mobile share sheet integration (Save to Gallery/Photos).
